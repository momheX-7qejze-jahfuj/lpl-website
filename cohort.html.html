The Curriculum

Your 12-Week Journey

Every week maps to one element of the P.R.E.S.S.U.R.E. System™. You complete one behavior workbook per week, make one specific commitment to the group, and track your follow-through on the live LPL™ Scoreboard. The curriculum is automotive-specific at every step — no translation required.

WK1
Foundation Phase

Perspective — Mindset Before Method

Your leadership audit. How do you actually see your role, your team, and your responsibility under pressure? We start here before installing anything else. Includes: Perspective Workbook + Day 1 Pressure Score™ Assessment (completed before this session).

WK2
Foundation Phase

Reality — The Honest Scoreboard

The gap between what you say as a leader and what your team actually experiences. CSI data. Turnover history. Coaching frequency. Reality is where coaching starts — and where most leaders have never been willing to look.

WK3
Foundation Phase

Execution — Installing the First Behaviors

The 8 Pressure Behaviors begin here. Clarity in communication. Coaching frequency. Pre-shift preparation. We identify your single highest-leverage behavior and begin installing it through the workbook system.

WK4
Installation Phase

Systems — Cadence That Outlasts the Coaching Session

Behavior change without a system is inspiration, not transformation. We install the weekly leadership cadence, accountability structure, and scoreboard rhythm that makes the change survive on a Tuesday morning when I'm not in the room.

WK5
Installation Phase

Standards — Define What Is Non-Negotiable

Culture is what you tolerate. This week you define what is acceptable, what is expected, and what is non-negotiable in your department. The difference between a high-retention store and a revolving door almost always lives in this conversation.

WK6
Mid-Program

Mid-Program Review + Behavior Recalibration

Group reset. Score review. Where are you versus Week 1? What behaviors are compounding? What is drifting? This session recalibrates priorities for the second half. Individual 1:1 sessions with Coach Joe this week for all members.

WK7
Performance Phase

Upgrade — Deliberate Practice and Continuous Growth

Leaders who stop growing stop leading. This week focuses on building a personal practice of deliberate leadership development — and teaching the leaders below you to do the same. This is where the system starts to multiply.

WK8
Performance Phase

Results — Connecting Behavior to Business Outcomes

What you measure moves. We identify each member's primary leading indicator — the single behavior most directly connected to their key business metric. Gross per unit. CSI. Show rates. Retention. These are not accidents. They are downstream of leadership behavior.

WK9
Performance Phase

Pressure Under Fire — Advanced Scenario Coaching

Live scenario-based group coaching using real situations members bring from their stores this week. Deal-desk breakdowns. Difficult team conversations. Performance management moments. The full P.R.E.S.S.U.R.E. framework applied to the situations happening right now in your world.

WK10
Legacy Phase

Elevate — Multiply Your Leadership

The best leaders don't just run great operations — they build great leaders who run great operations without them. This week focuses on developing the people below you using the same principles you've been installing. Your influence begins to compound.

WK11

Building Your Leadership Legacy

What does leadership look like in your store when you are not in the room? We build the structure, the standards, and the cultural architecture that answers that question definitively — and permanently. Your legacy plan is complete by end of session.

Final Pressure Score™ Assessment. Your before-and-after story. Your 90-day leadership case study — real data, real behavior change, real business impact. Group celebration. Forward commitment. The cohort closes. The alumni community begins. The next chapter starts here.

The Tracking System

Every week, you score yourself across three dimensions. These are not grades. They are mirrors. The scoreboard works because of honesty — and Coach Joe can see the patterns when scores don't match behavior. Protect the integrity of this room.

Consistency
Did I practice my leadership behavior consistently this week — or only when it was convenient?
Q

Did I execute the behavior effectively — or did I go through the motions without real intention?

What Coach Joe Expects

The Cohort Standards

These are not suggestions. They are the conditions that make this cohort work. When every member holds these standards, the group becomes the most valuable leadership development room in automotive retail. These are the non-negotiables.

1

Show Up. Every Week.

Sessions are 45 minutes and non-negotiable. A genuine conflict? Notify Coach Joe in advance, access the recording within 24 hours, and submit your weekly commitment through the member portal. Two unaddressed absences means forfeiting your seat — without refund.

2

One Commitment. Specific. Behavioral. Weekly.

Every session closes with a commitment — not a goal, a behavior. "I will conduct a 10-minute coaching conversation with each of my managers before noon every Monday this week." Specific enough to answer yes or no on follow-through. Vague commitments produce vague results. Every time.

3

Score Yourself Honestly.

The scoreboard produces insight only when scores reflect reality. A leader who scores a 3 and reflects honestly is building self-awareness. A leader who inflates their score is lying to themselves — and the group pays the cost of it in diminished accountability.

4

What Happens In The Lab, Stays In The Lab.

Members share real situations — real numbers, real failures, real breakthroughs. Confidentiality is the standard that makes depth possible. Names, situations, and specifics shared in sessions remain exclusively within the group. This is non-negotiable.

5

Protect Your 1:1 Sessions with Coach Joe.

One coaching session per month. Scheduling link provided in your onboarding email. Cancellations require 48-hour notice. The individual session is where your deepest development work happens. Give it the same respect you'd give your most important customer appointment.
