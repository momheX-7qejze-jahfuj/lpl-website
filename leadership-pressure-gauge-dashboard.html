The practice

The Self Check-in

Name what you feel. Locate it. Decide what comes next.
Step 1
Name it and plot it

Ask yourself the hard, simple questions. What am I feeling right now? How intense is it? Is it working in my favor, or limiting my behavior?

Find the spot on the matrix that tells the truth. Drop your marker there. The honest plot beats the flattering one.

Step 2
Is this serving me, or the people I lead?

Sit with that. Then ask the next one. What small shift would help me feel more empowered and move forward? Who, or what, could support that shift?

You do not need a new life. You need the next small move.

And mind the labels. The words you choose decide whether a feeling owns you or moves through you. Instead of "I'm anxious" try "I'm noticing feelings of anxiety." Instead of "I'm stressed" try "I'm feeling tension." That small shift turns the feeling into a temporary experience. Not something fused with who you are.
