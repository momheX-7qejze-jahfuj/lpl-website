About Coach Joe

Leadership built from
the inside out.

Joseph "Coach Joe" Clementi is not a consultant who studied automotive. He is an automotive leader who became a coach — because the field demanded a system that didn't exist yet.

The Story

Real experience.
Real language.
Real results.

Coach Joe spent over two decades inside automotive retail — as a manager, a director, and a leader who understood exactly what it felt like to stand at the write-up desk at 7pm on the last day of the month and watch the numbers fall short. Again. Not because of the market. Not because of the product. Because of a behavior in the management layer that nobody had coached. Ever. He has sat in that silence. He has hired the manager who looked perfect and left in 90 days. He has watched a CSI score not move despite every training program, every meeting, every conversation — because nobody was coaching the behavior behind the score. He built The P.R.E.S.S.U.R.E. System™ because that system did not exist when he needed it. And because the next GM who feels what he felt in that moment deserves a better answer than "try harder."

That experience became the foundation of The P.R.E.S.S.U.R.E. System™. Every element, every tool, and every workbook in the Leadership Pressure Lab™ was built inside the dealer environment — not adapted from a generic coaching model. It speaks the language of advisors, ELR, CSI, and month-end because it was designed there.

Coach Joe now works with dealer principals, general managers, service directors, and sales managers across the United States — helping them install the behavioral infrastructure that makes performance sustainable, not seasonal.

The metric you want is on the other side of the behavior you are not yet coaching consistently.
