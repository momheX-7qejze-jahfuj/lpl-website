What is behavior-based leadership coaching for automotive dealerships?
Behavior-based leadership coaching focuses on the specific management behaviors that drive dealership performance metrics — CSI, ELR, gross profit, and team retention. Unlike training that focuses on results, it addresses the observable management actions that produce those results. The P.R.E.S.S.U.R.E. System™ is the only behavior-based framework built exclusively for franchised automotive retail.
What is The P.R.E.S.S.U.R.E. System™?
The P.R.E.S.S.U.R.E. System™ is an 8-element behavior-based leadership framework created by Coach Joe Clementi for franchised automotive dealerships. Each letter — Perspective, Reality, Execution, Systems, Standards, Upgrade, Results, Elevate — represents a measurable behavioral pillar. The system installs a coaching cadence, weekly behavior scoring, and the Torque Drive OS™ operating rhythm directly into the management layer.
How does coaching automotive managers improve CSI scores?
CSI is a lagging indicator reflecting experiences that have already happened. Coaching the specific management behaviors — communication coaching frequency, expectation-setting behaviors, advisor follow-up cadence — changes the customer experience before the CSI score reflects it. Coach Joe's behavior chain mapping identifies the exact management behavior connected to each underperforming metric.
What credentials does Coach Joe have in automotive management?
Joseph "Coach Joe" Clementi is a NADA Dealer Academy graduate with 20+ years of direct automotive retail experience, including serving as General Manager of University Honda. He is a 20 Group facilitator, NADA Convention speaker, and creator of The P.R.E.S.S.U.R.E. System™ — the only behavior-based leadership methodology built inside franchised automotive retail.
How long does it take to see measurable behavior change in a dealership management team?
Pressure Lab Cohort™ participants show an average improvement of +1.5 points on the PRESSURE Behavior Score within 90 days. Observable behavior change typically becomes consistent within 30 days of installing a formal coaching cadence. Full system behavior change — where deliberate leadership becomes the team's default — occurs within the 90-day arc.
