Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Summary: The Leadership Pressure Lab™ (coachjoeleadership.com) is owned and operated by Joseph Clementi. We collect only the information necessary to provide our services and improve your experience. We do not sell your personal data. Period.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly — including your name, email address, job title, and dealership information when you subscribe to The Monday Brief, book a Strategy Call, or complete a contact form. We may also collect usage data including pages visited, time on site, and referring URLs through analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to: deliver The Monday Brief and other communications you have requested; respond to your inquiries; provide coaching services you have purchased; improve our website and content; and send relevant updates about The Leadership Pressure Lab™. We will never use your information for purposes beyond what you have consented to.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third party. We may share data with service providers (email platforms, payment processors, analytics) strictly for the purpose of operating our business. These partners are contractually bound to keep your data confidential.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience on our website — including analytics cookies (to understand how our site is used) and preference cookies (to remember your choices). You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Declining cookies will not prevent you from accessing our content.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal information we hold about you. You can unsubscribe from The Monday Brief at any time using the link in any email. To request data access or deletion, email us at joe@coachjoeleadership.com.
