What Pressure Reveals

Pressure exposes the leader you have already built.

Every service drive runs on pressure. Month-end. Turnover. The number on the board. Most leaders manage the number, and wonder why it never holds. This book hands you the system underneath it.

Who this is for

Service directors. Fixed operations managers. Shop foremen stepping into their first leadership seat. Anyone responsible for a number they cannot personally produce.

If you have ever hit the number one month and missed it the next with the same people and the same process, this book is about the gap in between.

What is inside

  • The TORQUE Method A repeatable system for turning intent into behavior that holds under load.
  • The Manager Archetypes Four default patterns leaders fall back on when pressure arrives. Find yours.
  • The Field Deck Ten cards you can run with your team on a Tuesday morning. No prep required.

About the author

Joseph Clementi has spent nearly thirty years in automotive retail leadership. He writes and coaches as Coach Joe and founded The Leadership Pressure Lab, where he works with fixed operations leaders on the behaviors that produce results instead of the results themselves.

He is also the author of Built by Repetition. He lives in the Greater Sacramento area.
